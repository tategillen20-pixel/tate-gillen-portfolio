Project overview

Structured research for investment screening

The scanner evaluates B2B SaaS companies against defined criteria and presents company and market intelligence in a consistent format for faster initial review.

Business problem

Early-stage company research is repetitive

Finding, validating, and comparing information across company sites and public sources takes time, while inconsistent research formats make targets harder to evaluate side by side.

Intended users

Investment and business research teams

The application supports users who screen software companies and need a clear first-pass view of business fit, market context, and areas that require deeper diligence.

Solution

Criteria-driven research with structured output

The scanner combines web research with AI-assisted synthesis to organize findings around the investment questions that matter, without replacing human investment judgment.

How the system works

A consistent four-step screening process

  1. 01

    Define the target

    Provide a company and relevant investment criteria.

  2. 02

    Gather research

    Collect business and market information from available public sources.

  3. 03

    Evaluate evidence

    Organize findings and use AI-assisted analysis to evaluate fit.

  4. 04

    Produce the brief

    Return a repeatable company summary, score, insights, and follow-up questions.

Core design challenge

Consistency across incomplete information

The project addresses source variability, separates evidence from synthesis, and converts a broad research task into a more repeatable screening workflow.

Key design decisions

Research structure before automation

01

Evidence before synthesis

Gather and organize source information before generating conclusions.

02

Repeatable structure

Present every company using the same evaluation format.

03

Transparent uncertainty

Identify incomplete information instead of presenting unsupported claims.

04

Human judgment remains central

The application supports initial screening but does not make investment decisions.

What I would build next

Deeper sourcing and comparison tools

Future work could add target queues, comparable-company views, source confidence indicators, saved criteria sets, exports, and integrations with existing research systems.
