Project overview

Tailored resumes without rebuilding from scratch

The application uses a reusable career profile to help users create focused resume content for specific roles while preserving accurate experience and accomplishments.

Business problem

Resume tailoring is valuable but repetitive

Job seekers repeatedly reorganize the same career information, risking inconsistent details and spending time on formatting instead of selecting the strongest relevant evidence.

Intended users

Job seekers managing multiple applications

The tool supports people who want a consistent source of truth for their career history and a guided process for adapting it to a job description.

Solution

A master profile with job-specific generation

Structured JSON stores reusable career information. The application combines that profile with role requirements to suggest relevant, tailored resume content for user review.

How the system works

A grounded four-step tailoring workflow

  1. 01

    Build the profile

    Create or update a structured master career profile.

  2. 02

    Add the role

    Add the target role and job-specific information.

  3. 03

    Tailor the evidence

    Use AI to identify relevant experience and produce a focused draft.

  4. 04

    Review the resume

    Review, edit, and improve the content before use.

Core design challenge

Personalization without invented experience

The workflow separates source facts from generated writing, keeps career information reusable, and encourages human review so outputs remain accurate and authentic.

Key design decisions

Accuracy stays at the center of the workflow

01

One source of truth

Keep career evidence in a reusable master profile instead of recreating it for every role.

02

Facts before phrasing

Separate verified experience from AI-assisted writing and presentation.

03

Job-specific selection

Tailor by choosing relevant evidence rather than inventing new qualifications.

04

Human review is required

Make validation and editing an explicit final step before use.

What I would build next

Better editing and application management

Future work could add version comparison, export templates, accomplishment prompts, job tracking, quality checks, and clearer traceability back to the master profile.
