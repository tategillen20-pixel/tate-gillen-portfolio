Mission Knowledge Graph

A tool that transforms unstructured organizational information into a visual knowledge graph of capabilities, systems, relationships, and operational gaps.

Project overview

Making organizational relationships visible

The tool converts narrative information into a navigable graph that helps users explore how capabilities, systems, and operational needs connect.

Business problem

Critical knowledge is buried in documents

Organizational information often lives in unstructured reports and notes, making relationships difficult to see and capability gaps difficult to discuss with a shared frame of reference.

Intended users

Strategy, operations, and mission teams

The experience is intended for users who need to understand a complex operating environment, trace dependencies, and identify questions for further analysis.

Solution

From narrative text to an explorable graph

NLP-assisted extraction identifies relevant entities and relationships, then NetworkX organizes them into a visual model that users can inspect and refine.

Core design challenge

Turning ambiguity into a useful model

The project addresses inconsistent terminology, relationship extraction, visual density, and the need to keep AI-derived connections reviewable by a person.

Key design decisions

A graph that remains understandable and reviewable

01

Typed entities

Separate capabilities, systems, people, and processes instead of treating every node equally.

02

Reviewable relationships

Keep extracted connections visible so users can question and refine them.

03

Clarity over density

Organize the graph around useful relationships rather than maximizing node count.

04

Gaps are prompts

Present potential gaps as areas for investigation, not definitive conclusions.

What I would build next

Validation, filtering, and collaborative refinement

Future versions could include source-level citations, confidence scores, graph filters, change tracking, collaborative annotation, and exports to other analysis tools.
