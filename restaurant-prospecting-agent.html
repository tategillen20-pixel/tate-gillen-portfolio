Restaurant Prospecting Agent

An AI-powered sales intelligence application that helps POS sales representatives identify new restaurant openings and research existing restaurant prospects.

Project overview

Finding stronger restaurant prospects faster

The application brings prospect discovery and research into one focused workflow so sales representatives can spend less time switching between sources and more time acting on useful information.

Business problem

Prospecting is fragmented and time-intensive

New restaurant openings can be difficult to identify early, while researching existing prospects often requires repetitive searches across maps, websites, and business listings.

Intended users

POS sales representatives and sales teams

The tool is designed for representatives who need a repeatable way to build restaurant prospect lists, understand each business, and prepare for relevant outreach.

Solution

A single AI-assisted research workflow

Users can identify potential openings, search known restaurants, and turn available place and web information into concise, structured prospect intelligence.

How the system works

A repeatable path from market to outreach

    Review the structured output and prioritize follow-up.

Core design challenge

Useful output from uneven source data

The workflow reduces manual source switching, turns inconsistent business information into a consistent format, and keeps the final result readable for a sales user.

Key design decisions

Built for practical sales research

01

Start with the territory

Keep discovery grounded in the market a representative actually owns.

02

Evidence before qualification

Organize place and opening information before assessing opportunity fit.

03

Consistent lead structure

Present every prospect in a repeatable format that is easy to scan.

04

Support the seller

Provide useful context without automating the relationship or outreach decision.

What I would build next

More signals and stronger workflow integration

Future versions could add scheduled market monitoring, CRM export, prospect scoring, duplicate detection, and feedback loops that improve prioritization.
